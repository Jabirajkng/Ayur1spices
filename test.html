//////////////////////// history of Ayurspices for about//////////////////////// The Story of Ayurspices: A Legacy of Excellence in Spice Exporting Ayurspices is not just a company; it’s a legacy, a story that has spanned generations, founded on a vision of excellence and a deep passion for delivering the finest spices. The journey of Ayurspices began in 1967, when the founder, the father of our current owner Saju, decided to create a business that would change the way people experienced spices. With a simple but groundbreaking idea, he started by sourcing cardamom directly from the local farmers, ensuring that each grain was of the highest quality. He carefully handpicked the finest cardamom and began selling it in a small shop in the heart of the town. What began as a modest venture quickly gained attention, with customers flocking to the shop for the unparalleled freshness and exceptional taste of the spices. This humble beginning marked the birth of Ayurspices, a company that would soon grow into a well-respected name in the spice industry. As the years passed, the legacy of Ayurspices was passed down to Saju, the sixth son of the founder. Saju took over the reins of the business, carrying forward the vision and values that his father had instilled in the company. Under his leadership, Ayurspices expanded its horizons beyond the local market and grew into one of the leading spice exporters in India. Saju’s commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction has been the driving force behind the company’s success. While cardamom remained a signature product, Ayurspices gradually expanded its offerings to include a wide variety of spices sourced from the best farms across India. These include black pepper, cinnamon, turmeric, nutmeg, nutmeg mace, and dried ginger, among others. Each spice is carefully selected for its unique flavor and quality, ensuring that Ayurspices remains synonymous with the best in the spice industry. A key factor in Ayurspices’ continued success is its close relationship with farmers across India. By sourcing directly from local farms, Ayurspices ensures that it receives the freshest spices at the most competitive prices. This direct relationship allows the company to offer premium quality spices while supporting the livelihoods of farmers and promoting fair trade practices. Through these partnerships, Ayurspices is able to maintain its high standards of quality, which have earned the company trust and loyalty from customers nationwide. Today, Ayurspices has a strong presence across India, with key operations in regions such as North Bangalore, Rajasthan, Kolhapur, Bihar, Delhi, and Odisha. These areas are home to some of India’s most renowned spice farms, where Ayurspices sources its premium products. By working directly with farmers in these regions, the company ensures that every product is fresh, authentic, and of the highest quality. Ayurspices’ growth has been nothing short of remarkable. What started as a small cardamom shop in 1967 has now grown into a thriving business with a turnover of 60 crores. The company’s commitment to quality, ethical sourcing, and customer satisfaction has established it as a leader in the spice export industry. Ayurspices is proud to have received numerous awards for excellence in exporting and for maintaining high standards in quality control. As we look toward the future, Ayurspices remains dedicated to the values that have defined the company from the very beginning. Our commitment to delivering the finest, freshest spices, supporting local farmers, and maintaining sustainable practices will continue to drive us forward. With a legacy of excellence behind us and a bright future ahead, Ayurspices is excited to continue serving our loyal customers and bringing the best of India’s spices to kitchens worldwide. Premium Dried Ginger

The unique, aromatic Dried Ginger from Rajakumari and surrounding regions is celebrated globally. Cultivated in the fertile highlands, it offers an exceptional pungent flavor and numerous health benefits, making it a sought-after spice among chefs, herbalists, and spice enthusiasts worldwide.

Rajakumari, along with regions like Biyaldam, Marayoor, Anayarangal, Kanthaloor, Chembothudukudi, and the fertile 80 acres is known for producing some of the finest Dried Ginger in the world. These regions’ unique geographical conditions, combined with sustainable farming practices, contribute to the exceptional quality of ginger grown here.

The high-altitude regions of Rajakumari and surrounding areas offer the ideal environment for cultivating potent, aromatic Dried Ginger. The cool temperatures, rich soil, and careful cultivation ensure that each piece of ginger retains its sharp, pungent flavor and rich medicinal value, making it a premium spice. The ginger grown here is highly valued by chefs and health-conscious individuals alike for its superior quality.

In the current market, finding naturally cultivated, chemical-free Dried Ginger is becoming increasingly rare. However, farmers in Rajakumari and nearby regions have maintained traditional, organic farming practices, ensuring that their ginger retains its authentic taste and health benefits. These farmers have spent years perfecting their agricultural techniques, focusing on soil rejuvenation and the careful selection of the best varieties to produce the finest quality ginger.

Shifting cultivation is an important practice in many of these regions, including Rajakumari and Marayoor. This traditional farming technique involves rotating farming plots over several years, allowing the soil to rest and regenerate naturally. By giving the land time to recover, farmers in these regions ensure that the soil remains fertile and that the ginger plants receive the nutrients they need for healthy growth. Shifting cultivation helps preserve the ecosystem and maintains the rich biodiversity of these highland areas.
